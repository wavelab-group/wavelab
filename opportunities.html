Join the WAVE Lab

We are looking for motivated M.S. and undergraduate students who want to understand how mechanical waves travel through matter, and to design materials that guide those waves exactly where we want them to go.

What you would work on

In the WAVE Lab, you will combine theory, simulation, and design to discover new ways of guiding, filtering, and controlling elastic waves. Projects sit at the intersection of mechanics, physics, and computation, and include:

  • Phononic crystals and elastic metamaterials
  • Nonlocal (beyond-nearest-neighbour) wave mechanics
  • Dispersion engineering and inverse design
  • Porous and architected materials with tailored dynamic response
  • Exotic wave phenomena such as nonreciprocity and fractal-like wave packets

You will build strong skills in modelling, numerical methods, and scientific communication, with mentoring toward publications and conference presentations.

Background we look for

  • B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, or a related field such as Aerospace, Civil, Engineering Physics, or Applied Mathematics
  • A solid foundation in mechanics, dynamics and vibrations
  • Comfort with linear algebra and differential equations
  • Curiosity, persistence, and enjoyment of hard problems

Experience that is a plus

  • Abaqus
  • COMSOL
  • MATLAB
  • Python

None of these is required. If you are eager to learn, we will help you get there.

Interested?

Send an email to spaul@mtech.edu with your CV, unofficial transcripts, and a few sentences about your research interests and why the WAVE Lab is a good fit for you.
