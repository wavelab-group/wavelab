WAVELab

Wave Analysis, Visualization, and Engineering Laboratory

We study how elastic and mechanical waves travel through architected and periodic materials, and we design new ways to guide, filter, and control them. Our work spans phononic and elastic metamaterials, wave-based computing, nonreciprocity and nonlocal wave mechanics, dispersion engineering, and the inverse design of structures with prescribed dynamic behavior — connecting fundamental wave physics to real engineering applications in vibration control, acoustic devices, and signal processing.
